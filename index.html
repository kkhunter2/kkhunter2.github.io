Kodi YouTube

Personal configuration of the YouTube add-on for Kodi

Kodi YouTube is a private, non-commercial setup that lets the developer sign in to their own YouTube account from the open-source YouTube add-on for Kodi on a home theater PC. It is used to browse subscriptions and play videos on a TV. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

개인 홈시어터 PC의 Kodi에서 본인 YouTube 계정으로 구독 목록을 보고 영상을 재생하기 위한 비상업적 개인용 설정입니다. 일반 사용자에게 제공하는 서비스가 아닙니다.

Google account access

When you sign in, the add-on requests access to your YouTube account through Google OAuth so it can show your subscriptions, playlists and watch history inside Kodi. See the Privacy Policy for how this data is handled.
