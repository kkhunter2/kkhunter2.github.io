Privacy Policy

Kodi YouTube · Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Kodi YouTube application ("the app") handles information when you sign in with your Google account.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to read your YouTube account data, such as subscriptions, playlists, liked videos and watch history, and to perform actions you request in Kodi, such as rating a video or adding it to a playlist.

Where the data goes

  • All data is requested directly from Google by the Kodi add-on running on your own device and is shown only on that device.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in Kodi's add-on data folder on your device.
  • The developer does not operate any server for this app and does not collect, store, sell or share your data with anyone.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and remove locally stored tokens by signing out in the add-on settings or deleting the add-on's data folder in Kodi.
